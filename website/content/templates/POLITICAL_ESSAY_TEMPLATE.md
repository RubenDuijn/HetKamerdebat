## Editorial safeguards checklist
- Facts, attributed statements and interpretation are separated.
- Predictions, warnings, promises and outcomes are explicitly distinguished.
- No claim that a politician predicted an event unless original wording supports it.
- Complex developments are not reduced to one law, treaty or single decision without evidence.
- Correlation, contribution and proven causation are explicitly separated.
- Relevant counterarguments and alternative explanations are included.
- Complete source register and claim-to-source mapping are included.
- Human editorial approval is required before publication.
